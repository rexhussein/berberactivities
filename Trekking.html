Trekking & Adventures

-->

This trek takes you through picturesque High Atlas mountain landscapes and charming villages, where you will experience warm hospitality, traditional food, and authentic customs. You will have the opportunity to interact with local families, learn about their way of life, and discover the cultural richness of rural Morocco. This short adventure is perfect for travelers who want to explore both the stunning natural beauty and the authentic culture of the Atlas Mountains. Itinerary

Day 1: Marrakesh – Imlil (1740m) – Tizi Mzik (2684m) – Tamsoulte Waterfalls (2300m) – Tizi Oussem (1850m)

After breakfast, we pick you up from your accommodation in Marrakesh and drive towards the High Atlas Mountains, passing through Tahanaout, Oued Ghighaya, and Asni. We arrive in Imlil (65 km from Marrakesh), where you will meet your guide and muleteer. Your trek begins with a steady ascent to the Tizi Mzik pass (2684m), offering spectacular views of Mount Toubkal and the Imlil Valley. We then continue to Tamsoulte, home to the highest waterfalls in the Atlas Mountains (around 100m). After enjoying the scenery and lunch, we descend through the beautiful juniper forests of the Azzaden Valley to reach Tizi Oussem village. Overnight in a traditional guesthouse. 🥾 Approx. 6 hours walking

Day 2: Tizi Oussem (1850m) – Id Issa (1800m) – Tizi Oudid (2000m) – Matat – Agersioual – Marrakesh

After breakfast, enjoy the peaceful morning atmosphere in this colorful valley surrounded by traditional Berber villages. We leave Tizi Oussem and descend to Id Issa village, then climb to the Tizi Oudid mountain pass (2000m). From there, we walk through juniper forests towards Matat village. Lunch will be served under walnut trees near the river — a perfect place to relax before continuing to Agersioual. Transfer back to Marrakesh in the afternoon (arrival around 17:00). 🥾 Approx. 5 hours walking.
